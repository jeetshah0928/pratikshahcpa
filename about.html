About the firm

Led by a CPA who has sat where you sit.

Pratik Shah founded this firm on a simple belief: business owners deserve a financial partner who understands strategy as well as compliance. With an MBA, a CPA license, and two decades advising businesses across a range of industries, Pratik built a practice that combines rigorous accounting with the judgment of an experienced advisor.

Proudly based in Murfreesboro, we serve clients throughout Middle Tennessee and, more broadly, across Tennessee, Kentucky, and Alabama — with the systems in place to support clients as they continue to expand into new markets.

Every client works directly with Pratik and a small, senior team — not a call center, and not a rotating cast of junior staff.
